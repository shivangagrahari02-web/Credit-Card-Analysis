Credit Card Financial Dashboard Analysis

Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end data analytics and business solution focused on credit card transaction and customer behavior analysis. The dashboard was developed using Power BI to provide real-time insights into revenue trends, customer segmentation, transaction patterns, and operational KPIs. The analysis helps stakeholders monitor business performance, identify revenue-driving customer groups, and support data-driven decision-making for credit card operations.

Project Objective

The objective of this project was to create an interactive weekly dashboard for credit card operations that delivers insights into essential performance metrics, customer behavior, transaction trends, and revenue patterns. The dashboard enables stakeholders to effectively track operational performance and identify growth opportunities.

Business Problem Statements

1. Revenue Performance Analysis

Analyze revenue generation across different customer groups, card categories, expenditure types, and transaction channels to identify key business drivers.

2. Customer Segmentation Analysis

Understand how demographic factors such as income group, occupation, education level, gender, and marital status influence transaction behavior and revenue contribution.

3. Transaction Trend Analysis

Track quarterly transaction volume, transaction amount, and revenue trends to evaluate operational consistency and business growth.

4. Card Category Performance

Evaluate the contribution of different credit card categories such as Blue, Silver, Gold, and Platinum toward overall business revenue and transaction volume.
